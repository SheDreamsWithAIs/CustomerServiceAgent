Title: OfficeLifeline Technical FAQ (Sample)

Where can I find data privacy settings?
- Navigate to Settings > Privacy in the top-right user menu. From there you can:
  - Export your data
  - Delete your account
  - Opt-in/out of analytics
  - Manage cookie preferences

How do I reset my password?
- Go to the login screen and click “Forgot password”. You’ll receive a reset link via email.

Why am I seeing a 401 Unauthorized error?
- Your token may be expired. Log out and back in, or refresh your API token in Settings > Developer.

How do I report a bug?
- Open a ticket at support@officelifeline.example with steps to reproduce, screenshots, and your browser version.

Rate limits for the API
- Default: 60 requests/minute per user. Enterprise plans can request higher limits via support.

Data retention
- Logs are retained for 30 days by default (Enterprise plans configurable up to 365 days).

Deployment troubleshooting
- If deploys fail, verify environment variables and check the build logs for missing secrets or incorrect API endpoints.
