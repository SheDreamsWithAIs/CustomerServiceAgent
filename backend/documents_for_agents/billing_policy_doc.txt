Title: OfficeLifeline Billing Policy (Sample)

Plans
- Basic: $0/month. Limited features; community support only.
- Pro: $29.99/month. Full features; standard support.
- Enterprise: Custom pricing. SLA and dedicated support available.

Invoices & Payment
- Billing cycle: Monthly on the signup day.
- Invoice ID format: INV-<4 digits> (e.g., INV-1001).
- Payment methods: Credit card; ACH (Enterprise only).

Grace Period & Dunning
- Grace period: 7 days after invoice due date.
- Dunning: Reminder emails at day 1, 4, and 7 of delinquency.
- Service limitation: Pro features paused after grace period until payment succeeds.

Refunds & Cancellations
- Pro-rated refunds: Not offered for monthly plans once the billing cycle starts.
- Cancellations: Effective at the end of the current billing period.
- Downgrades: Apply at the next billing cycle.

Proration
- Upgrades mid-cycle: Charged a proportional amount for remaining days in cycle.
- Downgrades mid-cycle: Take effect next cycle; no mid-cycle credit.

Taxes
- Taxes may apply depending on jurisdiction; shown on the invoice when applicable.

Disputes
- Payment disputes: Contact support within 14 days of the invoice date with the invoice ID.

Support Contacts
- Billing inquiries: billing@officelifeline.example
- General support: support@officelifeline.example

Notes
- Policy changes may occur; latest policy snapshot supersedes prior versions.
